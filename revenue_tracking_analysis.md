## Typical Revenue Tracking Methods in Restaurant Groups

### Method 1: Single Entity with Classes
- All businesses operate under one legal entity
- QuickBooks classes used to track each revenue center
- Shared expenses allocated using class assignments
- Single tax return, single set of books
- Relatively simple, but less detailed separation

### Method 2: Multiple Entities with Inter-company Transactions
- Separate legal entities for each concept
- Inter-company transactions to allocate shared resources
- More complex accounting but cleaner separation
- Multiple tax returns
- Better liability protection

### Method 3: Hybrid Approach
- Primary entity with sub-entities for certain operations
- Management company structure with service agreements
- Combines benefits of both approaches
- More sophisticated tracking capabilities
- Better scalability for future growth
