# Chubby Fish Client Requirements Analysis

## Services the Client WANTS Included

1. **Monthly financial reporting**: The client emphasized they need regular monthly reports to review with managers
2. **Strategic tax planning**: The client wants proactive tax advice, especially related to capital investments (cost segregation analysis for their $1M building investment)
3. **QuickBooks management**: Current CPAs aren't maintaining their QuickBooks properly (missing 3 months of revenue)
4. **Prime cost tracking**: Weekly financial analysis, particularly for food and labor costs
5. **Tax filing services**: Sales tax, Hospitality tax, Liquor tax filing and compliance
6. **Vendors/invoice reconciliation**: Especially tracking credits from vendors like Halperns
7. **Credit card reconciliation**: Making sure credit card charges match actual deliveries
8. **Multiple revenue stream tracking**: Client has revenue centers for: downstairs bar, private dining, Chubby Fish restaurant, and an Airbnb
9. **Data entry of invoices**: Client will send invoices for us to enter into the system

## Services the Client is NOT Interested In (or Handles Internally)

1. **Payroll processing**: Client's wife currently handles this and wants to maintain control
2. **Accounts payable execution**: Wife will continue to make payments, but wants us to handle invoice data entry
3. **Scheduling software**: Not needed as kitchen staffing is very consistent

## Current Systems & Pain Points

- Using QuickBooks but unhappy with current CPAs (Gerard Noel Russell) who "charge monthly, but don't do monthly work"
- Selida POS system (though company was acquired and may eventually shut down)
- Escalon for payroll
- South Atlantic bank for banking
- Food Tracker for food cost analysis (client enters invoices manually)
- No cost segregation analysis being done for tax benefits on their capital investments

## Client Priorities

The client is looking for a partner who can be responsive, provide timely reporting, and help them make better business decisions as they expand to multiple concepts. They want someone who feels like an in-house bookkeeper but without the full-time cost and turnover risk.
