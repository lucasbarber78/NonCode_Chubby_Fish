# Revenue Tracking Recommendations
## Chubby Fish Restaurant Group

Based on our initial conversation, we understand you operate multiple business units with distinct revenue streams:

- Chubby Fish restaurant
- Seahorse bar
- Private dining (currently tracked through Seahorse)
- Airbnb rental

Effective revenue tracking across these business units is crucial for making informed business decisions, especially as you look to expand with additional concepts in the future.

## Current Understanding

From our discussion, we understand:

- You have multiple revenue centers that you want to track separately
- Private dining revenue currently flows through the Seahorse revenue center via your POS
- You're using the Selido POS system with some customizations
- You conceptualize your business as having distinct revenue centers

## Current Challenges

Based on our conversation, we've identified several challenges with your current revenue tracking approach:

1. **Revenue Attribution**: Private dining revenue is currently attributed to Seahorse since it's processed through the same POS terminal

2. **System Separation**: While you conceptualize separate revenue centers, the technical implementation may not fully support this separation

3. **Manual Processes**: There appears to be significant manual work in tracking and attributing revenue

4. **Cross-Platform Integration**: The Airbnb rental likely uses a completely different system than your restaurant operations

## Recommended Approaches for Revenue Tracking

### Option 1: Enhanced POS Configuration
**Structure:**
- Configure your existing Selido POS for better business unit separation
- Set up distinct revenue tracking at point of sale
- Create customized reporting by business unit

**Benefits:**
- Works with your existing systems
- Minimal disruption to current operations
- Immediate improvement in data quality

**Implementation:**
- Reconfigure POS terminals for appropriate business unit attribution
- Set up distinct payment types or tender categories by business unit
- Create standard operating procedures for proper transaction coding
- Implement daily revenue reconciliation by business unit

### Option 2: Integrated Multi-Channel Solution
**Structure:**
- Maintain separate systems optimized for each business type (restaurant, rental)
- Implement middleware to consolidate data into accounting system
- Create consolidated reporting framework

**Benefits:**
- Each business uses tools designed for its specific operations
- Specialized reporting for each business type
- Flexible for future growth and new business units

**Implementation:**
- Configure dedicated POS setup for private dining
- Integrate Airbnb platform data with accounting system
- Establish data normalization standards across platforms
- Create consolidated reporting templates

### Option 3: Standalone Business Unit Approach
**Structure:**
- Treat each business unit as a separate operational entity
- Maintain dedicated systems for each operation
- Implement consolidated financial reporting

**Benefits:**
- Maximum business unit independence
- Cleaner operational separation
- Scalable for significant future growth

**Implementation:**
- Establish separate revenue management systems for each unit
- Create inter-unit transfer procedures for shared resources
- Implement master reporting system for ownership oversight
- Configure consolidated financial statements

## POS Integration Considerations

Based on your current use of Selido POS:

1. **Terminal Configuration**: Your current setup has private dining transactions processed through the bar terminal. We can explore options to better separate these transactions while maintaining operational efficiency.

2. **Menu Engineering**: Your POS menu structure can be configured to automatically assign transactions to the correct business unit based on item selection.

3. **Payment Processing**: Different payment types or routing can help distinguish between business units even when using shared terminals.

4. **Separate Systems Integration**: For your Airbnb rental, we can explore integration options between the Airbnb platform and your accounting system.
