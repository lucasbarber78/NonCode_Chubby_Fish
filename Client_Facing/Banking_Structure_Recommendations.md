## Banking Structure Options

### Option 1: Streamlined Approach
**Structure:**
- One primary operating account
- One payroll account
- One tax reserve account

**Best For:**
- Businesses with unified management and shared resources
- Operations where simplicity is valued over granular tracking
- Businesses with strong internal controls for allocation tracking

**Considerations:**
- Requires robust use of classes/departments in QuickBooks to track unit profitability
- Simplifies cash management and banking relationships
- May make it harder to isolate performance of individual business units

### Option 2: Business Unit Separation
**Structure:**
- Separate operating accounts for each business unit
- Shared payroll account
- Shared tax reserve account

**Best For:**
- Multi-concept operations where profitability tracking by unit is important
- Businesses where different units have different cash flow patterns
- Situations where you want to maintain separate "buckets" for each concept

**Considerations:**
- Balances granularity with practical management
- Provides clearer picture of each unit's performance
- Slightly more complex reconciliation process

### Option 3: Comprehensive Separation
**Structure:**
- Separate legal entities for each business
- Separate accounts for each entity and function
- Formal inter-company transaction tracking

**Best For:**
- Larger operations with distinct brands/concepts
- Situations with multiple ownership structures
- Operations seeking maximum liability protection

**Considerations:**
- Most complex to administer
- Highest level of legal and financial separation
- Most comprehensive for analysis and reporting
