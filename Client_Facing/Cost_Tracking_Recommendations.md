# Cost Tracking Recommendations
## Chubby Fish Restaurant Group

Based on our initial conversation, we've identified several opportunities to enhance cost tracking across your multiple business units. This document outlines our understanding of your current processes and provides recommendations tailored to your specific operations.

## Current Understanding

From our discussion, we understand:

- You operate multiple business units (Chubby Fish restaurant, Seahorse bar/private dining, Airbnb rental)
- You currently track food costs using Food Tracker with manual invoice entry
- You work with approximately 26 different vendors, including local fishermen
- You use American Express for many vendor payments
- You manage some specialized inventory items like caviar that require special tracking

## Cost Tracking Challenges in Restaurant Groups

Multi-concept restaurant operations like yours face unique challenges:

- **Cost Attribution**: Ensuring expenses are correctly assigned to the appropriate business unit
- **Shared Resources**: Allocating costs for resources used by multiple business units
- **Vendor Reconciliation**: Managing credits and adjustments across different concepts
- **Time Efficiency**: Minimizing manual data entry while maintaining accuracy
- **Performance Analysis**: Generating meaningful unit-level profitability reports

## Recommended Approaches

### Option 1: Enhanced Current Process
**Structure:**
- Continue using your current systems with added business unit tagging
- Implement consistent cost allocation rules for shared expenses
- Establish regular reconciliation processes for vendor credits

**Implementation:**
- Add business unit fields to your current invoice processing
- Develop standard allocation percentages for shared resources
- Create a tracking system for vendor credits and adjustments

### Option 2: Integrated System Approach
**Structure:**
- Implement an invoice processing system that integrates with your accounting software
- Set up automated business unit tagging based on vendor and item types
- Establish digital workflow for invoice approvals and coding

**Implementation:**
- Configure vendor defaults for recurring purchases
- Set up automatic splitting rules for shared expenses
- Implement digital capture for paper invoices
- Develop consistent allocation methodology

### Option 3: Restaurant-Specific Solution
**Structure:**
- Adopt a restaurant-specific inventory and cost management system
- Implement item-level tracking that maps to business units
- Integrate with POS for closed-loop cost analysis

**Implementation:**
- Map inventory items to business units and cost centers
- Configure automatic cost allocation based on usage
- Implement digital receiving and invoice matching
- Set up regular inventory counts by business unit

## Practical Next Steps

Regardless of which approach you choose, we recommend starting with:

1. **Document Current Process**: Create a complete map of how costs currently flow through your organization

2. **Standardize Vendor Management**: Establish consistent handling procedures for each vendor

3. **Develop Allocation Rules**: Create clear guidelines for how shared costs should be distributed

4. **Implement Credit Tracking**: Establish a system to ensure all vendor credits are captured and applied

5. **Prioritize High-Value Items**: Focus initial efforts on your highest-cost categories (seafood, etc.)

We would be happy to discuss these recommendations in more detail and help you implement the approach that best fits your operation.
