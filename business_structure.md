# Chubby Fish Business Structure and Plans

## Current Operations
- Chubby Fish restaurant (7 years in operation)
- Seahorse (bar and private dining, opened January 2025)
- Airbnb rental upstairs

## Business Approach
- Owner describes their strategy as "the long game" - planning for 35 years, not 3-5 years
- Focus on quality over cost-cutting (willing to pay overtime for best staff)
- Building leadership team and management structure to allow owner to step back from daily operations

## Future Plans
- Another concept planned for space behind current location
- Discussions with "a couple different prospects" for additional restaurants
- Owner investing in infrastructure to enable growth

## Staffing Philosophy
- Prioritizes hiring and retaining top talent
- Pays premium wages and overtime to maintain quality 
- Building career paths for kitchen staff to grow into management of new locations
- Owner quote: "Let's get the best people in here. Let's take care of them, and let's give them a position to grow into."

## Financial Considerations
- Recently invested approximately $1M in new building
- Seeking better financial structure to support expansion
- Needs better tools to manage across multiple revenue streams
- Looking for proactive tax strategy to maximize investment benefits
