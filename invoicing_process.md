# Chubby Fish Invoicing Process

## Current Vendor Landscape
- Client works with approximately 26 different purveyors
- Mix of corporate vendors with portals (Halperns, US Foods, etc.) and small local suppliers
- Some local fishermen with handwritten invoices
- Uses Amex for paying vendors like Limehouse and Halperns

## Current Pain Points
- Halperns credit card charging issues: they charge for full orders but sometimes ship partial orders
- Need for better reconciliation of credit card charges against actual deliveries
- Manual process for tracking credits and adjustments
- Client currently uses Food Tracker and manually enters invoice data

## Proposed Solution
- Client will send invoices to us for entry into QuickBooks
- We'll provide various options for invoice submission:
  - Mail/FedEx
  - Mobile photos/scans
  - Access to vendor portals where available
- We'll handle reconciliation of credit card charges against invoices
- We'll ensure credits are properly tracked and applied
- We'll track detailed item-level data to support analytics on food costs

## Special Considerations
- High-value items like caviar need careful tracking (client mentioned costs may jump 185% due to tariffs)
- Client sometimes excludes certain high-cost items from standard food cost calculations as they can skew metrics
- Multiple revenue streams need proper accounting attribution
