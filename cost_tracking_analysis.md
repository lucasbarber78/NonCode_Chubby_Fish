# Cost Tracking Analysis: Chubby Fish

## Current Cost Tracking Methods (Based on Transcript)

Based on our meeting with the client, we can identify the following about their current cost tracking approach:

### Vendor Management
- Using **American Express** for major vendor payments: "We do use AMEX a good bit for paying vendors...Limehouse is set up on an AMEX. Halperns is set up on an AMEX."
- Working with approximately **26 different purveyors** according to the client
- Dealing with **local fishermen** who provide handwritten invoices, requiring manual check writing: "fishermen we use who...just fish for us...there's no emailed invoice per se...Fish butcher in the morning, writing down all right, 10 pounds trigger fish, 14 pounds spade fish...weighing them out...cutting them a check."

### Invoice Processing
- **Manual data entry** into Food Tracker: "I use a food tracker. Okay, so I enter my invoices in on that. So, that's something that I've been doing since 2008, just to like for my own peace of mind."
- No apparent automation for assigning costs to specific business units
- Client mentioned significant time involvement: "By the time I finish uploading invoices...I may as well have just driven out to Folly."

### Cost Attribution
- No clear indication of how they attribute shared costs between business units
- For specialized items like caviar, the client mentioned excluding these from standard food cost calculations: "I'll subtract out that caviar cost just because it skews my numbers so much"
- Food costs appear to be tracked at a global level rather than by business unit

### Reconciliation Challenges
- Issues with vendor billing practices: "They run the card as soon as the order is placed...They ship eight caviar on the invoice that says 12. So we gotta scratch out 12 and say sent eight, but they've already rung us up for the entire 12." 
- Manual adjustment process for credits and discrepancies
- No systematic method for tracking credits mentioned

### Current System Limitations
- No indication of integration between Food Tracker and accounting system
- Manual processes taking significant owner time
- Lack of systematic allocation for shared resources between business units
- No mentioned strategy for overhead allocation

## Current vs. Best Practices Gap Analysis

| Area | Current Practice | Best Practice | Gap |
|------|------------------|---------------|-----|
| Cost Assignment | Appears to be global tracking without specific business unit allocation | Cost assignment by business unit at point of entry | Significant - no systematic allocation |
| Invoice Processing | Manual entry to Food Tracker by owner | Automated entry with validation and business unit tagging | High - labor intensive manual process |
| Vendor Management | Mix of AMEX payments and manual checks | Standardized vendor payment system with automated reconciliation | Medium - some structure exists |
| Credit Management | Manual tracking | Systematic credit tracking by vendor and business unit | High - potential for missed credits |
| Overhead Allocation | Not mentioned, likely ad hoc | Consistent allocation methodology with documented rationale | Unknown - needs investigation |

## Key Areas for Improvement

1. **Business Unit Cost Attribution**: Implement a systematic method for assigning costs to specific business units at the point of invoice entry

2. **Invoice Processing Efficiency**: Reduce manual data entry with automated systems

3. **Credit Management**: Implement a tracking system for vendor credits to ensure they're fully applied

4. **Allocation Methodology**: Develop a consistent approach for shared resource allocation between business units

5. **Integration**: Connect cost tracking with accounting system for comprehensive reporting
